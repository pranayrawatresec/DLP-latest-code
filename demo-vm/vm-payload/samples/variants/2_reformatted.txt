TIME WHILE ATTEMPTING TO CANALISE OUR ADVANCE INTO PRE-REGISTERED ARTILLERY ENGAGEMENT AREAS .

HOURS OF DARKNESS PRECEDING H-HOUR .

RELIEF IN PLACE BY 8 MOUNTAIN DIVISION .

BATTALION IS ATTACHED TO 14 MOUNTAIN BRIGADE . THE COMBAT ENGINEER COMPANY REVERTS TO DIVISIONAL

CONTROL ON COMPLETION OF THE BREACH AND PASSAGE OF LINES .

2 . MISSION .

14 MOUNTAIN BRIGADE SEIZES AND SECURES THE DOMINATING GROUND OVERLOOKING THE NORTHERN

APPROACHES TO THE LEH SECTOR NOT LATER THAN 0600 HOURS ON D-DAY, IN ORDER TO DENY THE ENEMY

OBSERVATION AND DIRECT FIRE ONTO THE MAIN SUPPLY ROUTE AND TO ENABLE THE FORWARD PASSAGE OF

DIVISIONAL RESERVES .

3 . EXECUTION .

A . COMMANDER’S INTENT . MY INTENT IS TO CLOSE WITH THE ENEMY ALONG THE WESTERN VALLEY WHILE

FIXING HIM IN THE EAST WITH DECEPTION AND HARASSING FIRES . THE DECISIVE OPERATION IS THE

SEIZURE OF OBJECTIVE FALCON BY THE MAIN EFFORT; SUPPORTING OPERATIONS ISOLATE THE OBJECTIVE AND

SECURE THE LINES OF COMMUNICATION . THE END STATE IS ACHIEVED WHEN THE DOMINATING GROUND IS

UNDER OUR CONTROL, THE MAIN SUPPLY ROUTE IS PROTECTED FROM DIRECT FIRE AND OBSERVATION, AND THE

BRIGADE IS POSTURED TO HAND OVER TO THE RELIEVING FORMATION WITHOUT A PAUSE IN THE DEFENCE .

B . CONCEPT OF OPERATIONS . THE OPERATION IS CONDUCTED IN THREE PHASES . PHASE ONE, SHAPING,

EXTENDS FROM D-2 TO H-HOUR AND COMPRISES RECONNAISSANCE, OBSTACLE REDUCTION, AND THE MOVEMENT

OF FORCES TO ATTACK POSITIONS UNDER COVER OF DARKNESS AND ELECTROMAGNETIC EMISSION CONTROL .

PHASE TWO, THE DECISIVE ACTION, BEGINS AT H-HOUR WITH A SYNCHRONISED ASSAULT ON OBJECTIVE

FALCON SUPPORTED BY PREPARATORY ARTILLERY FIRES AND CLOSE AIR SUPPORT ON CALL . PHASE THREE,

CONSOLIDATION, BEGINS ON SEIZURE OF THE OBJECTIVE AND INCLUDES REORGANISATION, CASUALTY

EVACUATION, RESUPPLY, AND PREPARATION FOR THE RELIEF IN PLACE .

C . SCHEME OF MANOEUVRE . THE MAIN EFFORT ADVANCES ALONG THE WESTERN AXIS WITH TWO ASSAULT

BATTALIONS FORWARD AND ONE IN DEPTH . THE MECHANISED BATTALION CONDUCTS A SUPPORTING ATTACK
