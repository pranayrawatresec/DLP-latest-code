Reactor Core Safety Assessment — Fuel Assembly Integrity Review

This assessment records the periodic evaluation of fuel assembly integrity for the
pressurised water reactor at the naval propulsion test facility. Coolant loop pressure,
primary circuit temperature margins and neutron flux distribution were measured across
three consecutive fuel cycles. Cladding oxidation was within the acceptance envelope for
all sampled assemblies. Control rod drive mechanism response times remained inside the
qualified band. The criticality safety analysis was re-run against the revised burnup
credit methodology and the subcritical margin was confirmed under all credible upset
conditions. Radiological shielding surveys around the containment boundary recorded dose
rates consistent with the design basis. Recommendations: continue quarterly assembly
inspection, revalidate the emergency core cooling injection setpoints, and schedule the
next full criticality re-analysis before the following refuelling outage.
